# Twelve-Week DeepML Motion/Animation Curriculum

| Week | Theme | Reading | Applied work |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Language identity, transforms, and evidence | Chapters 1–2 | Trace source to runtime for twenty foundation programs. |
| 2 | Skeletons, joints, bind poses, and topology | Chapter 3 | Build one valid rig and a topology diagnostic set. |
| 3 | Poses, clips, channels, keyframes, and interpolation | Chapters 4–5 | Author and sample a bounded clip library. |
| 4 | Curves, compression, blends, masks, and tolerances | Chapters 6–7 | Publish normalization and compression evidence. |
| 5 | State machines, IK, constraints, and scheduling | Chapters 8–9 | Build a controller with explicit failure behavior. |
| 6 | Retargeting, root motion, and timeline synchronization | Chapters 10–11 | Retarget and synchronize one locomotion sequence. |
| 7 | Procedural motion, matching, and trajectory prediction | Chapters 12–13 | Create a deterministic local-database motion query. |
| 8 | Locomotion, contacts, and physics-aware recovery | Chapters 14–15 | Measure slide, contact, handoff, and recovery criteria. |
| 9 | Portal, cinematic, and crowd motion systems | Chapters 16–17 | Design one integrated sequence and one scalable replay test. |
| 10 | Compiler optimization and equivalence | Chapter 18 | Prove one optimization under an explicit tolerance profile. |
| 11 | Diagnostics, R12/MCRT, and certification | Chapters 19–20 | Run a stage-aware negative and holdout campaign. |
| 12 | 8S profile and capstone toolchain | Chapters 21–22 | Publish a bounded standalone implementation plan and release manifest. |

## Assessment

- 20% weekly laboratories
- 20% negative-example and diagnostic portfolio
- 20% compiler/runtime evidence notebook
- 40% standalone toolchain capstone and conformance report

A passing capstone must disclose unsupported features and must not present modeled corpus outcomes as native execution evidence.
