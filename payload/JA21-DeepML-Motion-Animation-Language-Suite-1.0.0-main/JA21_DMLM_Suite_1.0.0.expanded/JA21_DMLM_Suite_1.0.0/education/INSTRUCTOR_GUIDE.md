
# DeepML Motion/Animation Instructor Guide

## Teaching principle

Teach every feature as a chain of motion claims and evidence. Syntax is introduced together with hierarchy, coordinate, time, solver, policy, diagnostic, lowering, and replay behavior.

## Recommended rhythm

1. Read one small positive program.
2. Inspect its corpus record, hashes, expected stage, and motion relation.
3. Compare a negative program on the same topic.
4. Identify the earliest responsible rejection stage.
5. Inspect R12/MCRT evidence and replay requirements.
6. Complete a laboratory and publish a concise evidence note.

## Grading rubric

| Dimension | Excellent | Adequate | Needs revision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motion semantics | Preserves hierarchy, spaces, timing, constraints, contacts, and state | Mostly correct with minor omissions | Relies on appearance or unstated assumptions |
| Evidence | Uses IDs, hashes, stages, tolerances, R12/MCRT, and replay context | Uses partial evidence | Relies on unverified narrative claims |
| Diagnostics | Identifies the root stage and bounded repair | Correct stage but limited explanation | Generic or downstream-only error |
| Claim boundaries | Separates modeled, structural, rendered, and native evidence | Minor ambiguity | Presents modeled outcomes as executed proof |
| Publication | Navigable, versioned, path-safe, and integrity-checked | Usable but incomplete | Missing structure, provenance, or limitations |

## Certification handling

Keep certification expected outcomes separate during ordinary laboratories. Freeze the implementation before holdout evaluation and disclose any prior exposure.
